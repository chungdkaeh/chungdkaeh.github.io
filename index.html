Privacy Policy for YouSee

Official user privacy disclosure for YouSee mobile application and related services.

Effective Date: September 29, 2026
Last Updated: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the YouSee mobile applications for Android and iOS devices, web browser experiences, and related communication services (collectively, the "Application" or "Services"), operated by Systematic Functions Joint Stock Company (hereinafter referred to as the "Service Provider", "we", "us", or "our").

We are dedicated to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding how your data is collected, utilized, shared, and protected. This policy is structured to comply with international regulatory benchmarks, including the Google Play Developer Policy, the Apple App Store Review Guidelines (Guideline 5.1.1), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA).

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide to Us

  • Account & Profile Information: When you register, log in, or configure your profile, we may collect your name, username, email address, phone number, and avatar/profile picture.
  • Communications & User Content: We process your instant messages, voice notes, photos, video recordings, and attached documents shared within conversation channels. Real-time audio and video communications are transmitted via secure protocols.
  • Customer Inquiries & Feedback: When reaching out to us at tienld@authenticeducationhub.com, we record your email address and message contents to assist you.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device & System Specifications: Device model, operating system version, hardware identifiers, language settings, and current application version.
  • Diagnostic & Telemetry Data: Crash reports, connection latency statistics, performance metrics, and error stack traces utilized to maintain service stability.
  • Network & IP Address: Internet Protocol (IP) address, network provider, and connection type (Wi-Fi or cellular) required to route media streams and establish peer-to-peer/relayed WebRTC communication sessions.

C. Artificial Intelligence (AI) Processing

The Application utilizes Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to enhance user experience, power automated communication assistance, and optimize real-time interaction features. All AI operations are performed strictly in compliance with this Privacy Policy and applicable data protection regulations. We do not sell or monetize personal data processed by AI features.

3. Third-Party Services and SDKs

We work with trusted third-party providers to deliver essential infrastructure:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase & Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Provides push notification infrastructure, crash detection, and aggregated analytics. Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • WebRTC / LiveKit: Delivers low-latency real-time voice and video communication channels.
  • Apple Push Notification service (APNs) & PushKit: Rings incoming calls via Apple CallKit and delivers iOS notifications.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party data brokers.

4. Data Retention Policy

  • Account Information: Retained for the duration of your active account plus up to 12 months after deactivation, unless a longer retention duration is required by law.
  • Technical & Diagnostic Logs: Retained for up to 24 months from collection to maintain reliability and security.
  • Aggregated Data: Anonymized metrics may be kept indefinitely for platform improvements.

5. Account & Data Deletion (Google Play & Apple Compliant)

You have the right to delete your account and all associated personal data at any time through either of the following methods:

  1. In-App Deletion: Open the YouSee app and navigate to: Settings > Account > Delete Account.
  2. Email Request: Send an email from your registered email address to tienld@authenticeducationhub.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request - YouSee App".

All personal information, message history, and profile data will be permanently wiped or anonymized within 30 days of verification.

6. Security and International Transfers

We employ industry-leading safeguards including TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, SRTP for audio/video media, and encrypted local storage for credentials. Where data is transferred internationally outside your country of residence, we implement European Commission-approved Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and equivalent protective frameworks.

7. Children's Privacy

YouSee is not intended for or directed towards children under sixteen (16) years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at tienld@authenticeducationhub.com for immediate removal.
